# Code of conduct

Help make Sheco a welcoming project for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces.

- Treat people with respect. Critique ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Be clear, constructive, and patient. Accept corrections and respect differences of opinion.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish another person's private information.
- Keep contributions and conversations relevant to the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to [shecof@ya.ru](mailto:shecof@ya.ru). Include enough context
to understand the concern, and avoid sharing unrelated private information. The maintainer will
review reports and may remove content, ask participants to change their behavior, or restrict
participation according to the circumstances. Reports will be handled privately as far as practical.
